    Apam SHS to be Upgraded from Category B to Category A

    9 August 2026

    The Minister for Education, Haruna Iddrisu, has announced plans to upgrade Apam Senior High School from a Category B school to a Category A institution.

    The planned upgrade will include infrastructure expansion, renovation and other development interventions to improve teaching and learning at the school.

    The government also plans to work towards completing abandoned GETFund projects at the school and provide a school bus to support transportation needs.

    Apam SHS Finishes Second in NSMQ 2026 Regional Qualifier

    25 July 2026

    Apam Senior High School placed second in the opening Central Region qualifier of the 2026 National Science and Maths Quiz.

    Apam SHS scored 39 points, finishing behind Mfantsiman Girls SHS, which scored 61 points.
